VIDEO 1 — HERO (Exploded Burger Assembly)
Start with every burger ingredient floating separately across the frame:
Sesame bun, sauce, fresh lettuce, tomato, red onion, avocado, cheddar cheese, grilled beef patty, crispy chicken layer, smoked bacon, pickles, bottom bun.

The camera slowly pushes forward. As the camera moves, every ingredient elegantly aligns itself. Nothing snaps. Everything floats gracefully. Tiny crumbs, tiny sauce particles, micro seasoning. Slow motion. Finally the burger completes itself into one premium BR-Burger signature burger. The burger slowly rotates 20 degrees. Camera stops.

End frame: Perfect completed burger centered.

VIDEO 2 — Burger transforms into Chicken Taco
Begin exactly where previous video ended: completed burger floating.

Camera slowly circles around it. As camera rotates, the burger gently disassembles. Ingredients stretch into flowing ribbons. Those ribbons morph into a premium crispy chicken taco: golden tortilla, crunchy fried chicken, purple cabbage, fresh lettuce, chipotle sauce, cheddar, tomatoes. Everything assembles mid-air. No cuts. Only continuous transformation.

End frame: Luxury chicken taco floating in center.

VIDEO 3 — Taco transforms into Cold Drink + Fries

The taco dissolves into flowing food particles. Particles swirl around and become premium crispy fries. Then fries rotate around a floating branded cold drink. Large condensation droplets, ice cubes rotating inside drink, tiny bubbles, golden fries, soft reflections. Premium fast-food commercial.

End frame: Drink center, fries orbiting around it.

BR-Burger Website Specification (Claude Code Prompt) Website Brief · Next.js / GSAP ScrollTrigger / Lenis

Master developer prompt instructing Claude Code to build BR-Burger with Next.js App Router, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis, living multi-colored typography, and 7 scroll-scrubbed video sections.

You are an award-winning creative developer.

You think like the teams behind Apple, Active Theory, Bruno Simon, Resn, Locomotive, Dogstudio and Awwwards Site of the Day winners.

Your task is to build a world-class cinematic website for a fictional premium fast food brand named

BR-Burger

The website must NOT feel like an ecommerce website. It should feel like an interactive movie where the entire experience is controlled by scrolling with buttery-smooth physics. No generic sections, no bootstrap grids, no cards everywhere. This should feel like a luxury product launch.

==================================================
1. TECH STACK
==================================================
- Next.js latest (App Router)
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- GSAP 3 & GSAP ScrollTrigger
- Lenis smooth scroll
- Framer Motion
- React Three Fiber (@react-three/fiber) & Drei (@react-three/drei)
- MotionPathPlugin
- SplitType
- React Icons

==================================================
2. DESIGN SYSTEM & PALETTE
==================================================
- Minimal, premium, luxury editorial (Apple keynote meets Nike campaign meets luxury food commercial).
- Base Background: Soft warm cream (#F8F6F2) and Warm White (#FCFBF8) with sections subtly shifting to warm beige, soft orange, and soft peach.
- Brand Colors:
  * Cream: #F8F6F2
  * Warm White: #FCFBF8
  * Orange: #FF8A00
  * Golden: #FFC44D
  * Tomato: #FF5630
  * Leaf Green: #43A047
  * Deep Charcoal: #1B1B1B
  * Brown: #5C4033
- Typography: Clash Display / General Sans for huge headlines, Satoshi / Inter for body and micro labels.

==================================================
3. LIVING, MULTI-COLORED TYPOGRAPHY
==================================================
- Typography must feel alive: split into characters using SplitType, with characters independently rotating, scaling, sliding, fading, and reacting to scroll velocity.
- Multi-colored artistic headline styling:
  * "CRAFTED" (orange) / "TO" (thin outline) / "PERFECTION" (gold)
  * "DOUBLE" (orange) / "CHEESE" (gold) / "BURGER" (charcoal)
  * "Crispy" (orange gradient) / "Chicken" (gold gradient) / "Taco" (green)
  * "Fresh" (green) / "Ingredients" (orange)
  * "Perfect" (cream) / "Every" (gold) / "Bite" (tomato)
- Layered editorial typography: giant 200px watermark outline text behind food, vertical labels, ultra-thin and bold pairings.

==================================================
4. VIDEO SYSTEM & SCROLL-SCRUBBING
==================================================
Videos stored in `assets/videos/`:
- `01-hero-burger.mp4`
- `02-taco.mp4`
- `03-drink-fries.mp4`
- `04-packaging.mp4`
- `05-menu.mp4`
- `06-ingredients.mp4`
- `07-finale.mp4`

All videos fill the viewport with pinned GSAP ScrollTrigger sections. Video progress (`video.currentTime`) is strictly bound to scroll progress. No autoplay, no native controls, no audio.
