VIDEO 1 — Hero Reveal (10–12 sec)
Prompt
Cinematic luxury fashion advertisement for BR Fashion.
Begin with a close-up of textured black concrete illuminated by subtle cyan and magenta neon. Oversized typography slowly fades into view as the camera dollies forward through drifting fog. A premium streetwear model emerges wearing an oversized hoodie, cargo pants, layered silver jewelry, and luxury sneakers. The camera performs smooth cinematic orbit shots, slow push-ins, and dramatic low-angle tracking. Neon reflections shimmer across wet pavement while floating dust catches the light. End on the complete BR Fashion campaign with the tagline Future Streetwear Collection. Hyper-realistic, premium editorial lighting, Vogue-level fashion commercial, seamless camera motion, 4K, 24fps.

VIDEO 2 — Product Showcase (Shirts • Tees • Pants)
Prompt
Create a high-end fashion commercial for BR Fashion showcasing three product categories with seamless cinematic transitions. Start with oversized graphic tees suspended in mid-air, rotating slowly before assembling onto a model. Transition into premium shirts through dynamic cloth simulations and stylish motion blur. Finish with cargo pants appearing through particle effects while the camera performs dramatic orbit shots and slow-motion close-ups highlighting fabric texture, stitching, and premium craftsmanship. Neon cyan and magenta lighting, industrial concrete environment, subtle fog, floating dust, editorial fashion cinematography, luxury advertising quality, hyper-realistic cloth physics, perfect for a scroll-animated website.

VIDEO 3 — Brand Film (Scroll Finale)
Prompt
Produce a cinematic brand film for BR Fashion inside a futuristic urban environment. Begin with wide drone-like shots of brutalist architecture before transitioning into dynamic tracking shots following fashion models walking confidently through neon-lit streets. Introduce oversized typography integrated naturally into buildings while cinematic slow-motion sequences highlight hoodies, tees, shirts, cargo pants, and sneakers. Include dramatic camera cranes, seamless whip-pan transitions, atmospheric fog, glowing cyan and magenta lights, reflective wet surfaces, floating particles, and premium editorial grading. Finish with a slow cinematic pull-back revealing the complete BR Fashion identity and the tagline Own The Street. Hyper-realistic luxury fashion commercial, 4K HDR, award-winning advertising aesthetic, designed specifically for scroll-driven interactive websites.

2. Claude code/Antigravity prompt:- You are an award-winning creative developer specializing in Awwwards Site of the Day experiences.
Build a premium, scroll-driven fashion website for BR Fashion, inspired by modern luxury streetwear editorials, cyberpunk lighting, oversized typography, and brutalist architecture.

Motion Principles
Luxury brands move slowly.
Use:
* long easing curves
* premium inertia
* cinematic timing
* elegant transitions

Avoid:
bouncing
elastic animations
excessive spinning
cartoon motion

Performance
* Lazy load all images and videos.
* Use next/image for images.
* Preload only the first hero image and critical fonts.
* Use GPU-friendly transforms (transform, opacity) instead of layout-changing properties.
* Decode videos only when they approach the viewport.
* Use ScrollTrigger matchMedia for responsive animation logic.
* Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
* Optimize for Lighthouse scores above 95.

Final Goal
Create an immersive, award-worthy fashion website where the provided campaign images establish the visual narrative and the three product videos are revealed through scroll-controlled storytelling. The experience should feel like an interactive luxury fashion editorial—every section introducing a new composition, with no autoplaying background videos, fluid 60 FPS motion, and a cohesive visual identity from start to finish.
