# Prompt 1 – 3D Exploded Kitchen (Recommended)

Reference: Use the uploaded kitchen image as the final layout. Preserve the exact cabinet dimensions, island proportions, marble pattern, lighting positions, window placement, refrigerator, cooktop, pendant lights, shelves, décor, and color palette.

---

### Prompt

Create an ultra-realistic 3D exploded architectural visualization of this luxury modern kitchen.

The camera remains perfectly locked with:
- no pan
- no zoom
- no orbit
- no handheld motion
- no lens distortion

The entire animation is designed for a scroll-controlled website, so movement should be extremely smooth and slow.

The animation begins with the completed kitchen.
Then every architectural component separates slightly from its neighboring components while maintaining perfect alignment.

Explosion sequence:

0–2 sec
Cabinet doors move forward a few centimeters.
Drawer fronts separate.
Shelves extend slightly.
Glass cabinets float outward.

---

2–4 sec
Upper cabinets lift upward.
Lower cabinets move downward.
Countertop floats upward.
Backsplash separates from the wall.

---

4–6 sec
Kitchen island separates into layers:
- marble slab
- support structure
- storage compartments
- decorative fluted panels
Each layer floats independently.

---

6–8 sec
Pendant lights rise.
Sink lifts.
Faucet separates.
Cooktop floats.
Chimney hood moves slightly away from the wall.
Plants, jars and decorative items lift subtly.

---

8–10 sec
Everything smoothly returns to its original position with precise alignment.
Warm ambient LEDs illuminate gradually.
End with the identical kitchen shown in the reference image.

Movement should feel like invisible magnetic forces.
No fast motion.
No bouncing.
No spinning.
Every object keeps its original orientation.

Photorealistic.
Luxury architectural visualization.
Octane Render quality.
8K.
HDR.
60 fps.

## My Recommendation for the Website Flow

To create a memorable experience, each section should introduce a different type of motion rather than repeating the same effect:

Section 1 (Hero): Kitchen assembles from an empty room ("We build spaces.")
Section 2: 3D exploded view of the completed kitchen ("See how it's crafted.")
Section 3: Zoom into premium details — marble veins, hardware, wood grain ("Experience the quality.")
Section 4: Before → After transformation ("See the impact of the design.")
Section 5: Portfolio gallery with cinematic transitions (Showcase completed projects.)
