THE PRANA SEQUENCE Website Specification (Claude Code Prompt) Website Brief · Next.js 15 / GSAP ScrollTrigger / Lenis

Master developer prompt instructing Claude Code to build THE PRANA SEQUENCE with Next.js 15 App Router, GSAP ScrollTrigger frame-by-frame scrubbing, Lenis, SplitType typography, and breathing rhythm choreography.

You are an award-winning frontend engineer and creative developer specializing in Awwwards-level experiences.

Build a premium cinematic website called

THE PRANA SEQUENCE
(Surya Namaskar)

The website should feel like an interactive meditation instead of a traditional website.

==================================================
1. TECH STACK & DEPENDENCIES
==================================================
• Next.js 15 App Router
• React 19
• TypeScript
• TailwindCSS
• GSAP 3 & GSAP ScrollTrigger
• Lenis Smooth Scroll
• Framer Motion
• SplitType
• No animation libraries except GSAP & Framer Motion.

==================================================
2. DESIGN LANGUAGE & COLOR PALETTE
==================================================
- Aesthetic: Minimal, Luxury, Indian Heritage, Editorial, Breathing, Natural, Warm.
- No glassmorphism, no neon, no harsh gradients.
- Generous whitespace, warm ivory backgrounds, soft shadows, large editorial typography.
- Typography: PP Editorial / Canela style headings paired with Inter body copy.
- Palette:
  * Warm Ivory: #F8F4EE
  * Sand / Gold Light: #F2E4C8
  * Turmeric Gold: #D6A95D
  * Raw Terracotta / Ochre: #B46A2B
  * Deep Charcoal: #2D241F
  * Accent: Warm Saffron

==================================================
3. SCROLL EXPERIENCE & MEDITATIVE PACING
==================================================
The entire website should feel synchronized with breathing. Every scroll movement is slow and deliberate. Sections fade smoothly into one another using long pinned sections and buttery easing curves.

==================================================
4. SECTION-BY-SECTION BUILD SPEC
==================================================

HOME / HERO:
- Media: `Practitioner_performing_Surya_Namaskar.mp4` (`hero-video-1`).
- Pinned section where video covers the viewport.
- Large editorial overlay: "THE PRANA SEQUENCE" with subheading "Authentic Yoga rooted in Indian Tradition."
- Breathing Circle below hero expanding and contracting continuously.
- On scroll, video scrubs frame-by-frame using GSAP ScrollTrigger without autoplay after leaving hero.

SECTION 2 — ABOUT & PHILOSOPHY:
- Pinned section using `Yogis_performing_Surya_Namaskar.mp4`.
- Parallax text blocks: Left heading "Ancient Wisdom", Right body explaining Prana & Surya Namaskar.
- Line-by-line paragraph reveals on scroll with subtle upward word fade.
- Video scales slowly from 1.15 to 1.0 during the scroll sequence.

SECTION 3 — TEACHER TRAINING:
- Minimal cream cards: "200 Hour", "300 Hour", "500 Hour".
- Cards stagger upward while scrolling with soft lift hover, gentle shadow, and image zoom.

SECTION 4 — RETREATS:
- Pinned split layout using `Yoga_retreat_at_sunrise.mp4`.
- Left: Huge heading "RETREATS".
- Right: Large scaling video with overlapping foreground cards sliding across ("Himalayan Retreat", "River Meditation", "Sunrise Immersion").

SECTION 5 — WORKSHOPS:
- Dark warm background using `Yoga_teacher_guiding_students.mp4`.
- Horizontal scrolling cards ("Breathwork", "Alignment", "Meditation") with 1° hover rotation.

SECTION 6 — TEMPLE TRANSITION:
- Pinned full-viewport exploding temple architecture (`Temple_architecture_separates.mp4`).
- SplitType quote animates independently: "The body is your temple. Breath is your guide."

SECTION 7 — CONTACT & FINALE:
- Still image `Yoga_retreat_journey_at_sunrise.jpeg` with large breathing CTA "Begin Your Practice".
- Button "Book Your Journey" with soft glow, slight scale, and fill animation.
- Minimal footer with Instagram, Email, Location.

==================================================
5. PERFORMANCE & ARCHITECTURE
==================================================
- Reusable React components: `Hero.tsx`, `About.tsx`, `Courses.tsx`, `Retreats.tsx`, `Workshops.tsx`, `Temple.tsx`, `Contact.tsx`, `Loader.tsx`, `Navbar.tsx`, `Footer.tsx`.
- Hooks: `useLenis.ts`, `useScrollAnimations.ts`.
- Video preloading only for hero; lazy loading for subsequent video assets.
