VIDEO 1 — HERO (The Jewel Box)
Start with a closed luxury mithai box floating in the maroon void: deep maroon lacquer finish, embossed gold paisley pattern, gold "BR-Mithai" monogram on the lid, a silk tassel hanging from the side.

The camera slowly pushes in. A thin line of warm golden light leaks from the seam of the lid. The lid lifts slowly like a jewellery case, hinged at the back. Warm light and golden dust spill out. Inside, rows of sweets sit on gold-foil cups in velvet compartments.

End frame: Box fully open, camera looking down into it at 45 degrees.

---

VIDEO 2 — Kaju Katli Levitation
Begin exactly where previous video ended: open box seen from 45 degrees.

Camera dives into the box. A single diamond-shaped kaju katli rises from its compartment and floats toward the camera. It rotates slowly in 3D. The silver vark on top crinkles and glints as light passes over it. Five more katli rise behind it and form a slow-rotating diamond constellation.

End frame: Hero kaju katli centered, constellation behind it.

---

VIDEO 3 — Motichoor Ladoo Burst
Begin with the kaju katli constellation. Camera orbits to the right.

The katli dissolve into golden particles. The particles swirl into hundreds of tiny saffron-orange boondi pearls, which pack themselves into a perfect round motichoor ladoo. A single pistachio sliver and a strand of saffron land on top. The ladoo turns slowly, ghee sheen highlighting each pearl.

End frame: Motichoor ladoo centered with boondi pearls orbiting it.

---

VIDEO 4 — Jalebi Spiral
Begin with the ladoo and orbiting pearls. Camera moves downward.

The pearls stretch into a glowing orange ribbon that coils into a jalebi spiral drawn in mid-air. Warm golden sugar syrup coats the spiral in slow motion, droplets glistening. The jalebi rotates like a galaxy.

End frame: Jalebi spiral face-on, filling the center of the frame.

---

VIDEO 5 — The Diya Constellation
Begin with the jalebi spiral. Camera pulls back slowly.

The spiral's glow separates into dozens of small flames. Each flame settles into a hand-painted clay diya. The diyas arrange themselves in a large rangoli pattern on an invisible floor beneath the camera: lotus, paisley and concentric circles. Marigold petals drift down through the scene.

End frame: Top-down view of the glowing diya rangoli.

---

VIDEO 6 — The Collection
Begin with the top-down diya rangoli. Camera tilts up to eye level.

From the center of the rangoli, the full BR-Mithai collection rises on floating gold-leaf pedestals: kaju katli, motichoor ladoo, jalebi, gulab jamun with a syrup glaze, rasgulla, soan papdi cubes, pista barfi, kesar peda, and dry-fruit rolls. Each sweet slowly rotates on its pedestal, arranged in a gentle arc like a jewellery display.

End frame: Arc of sweets on pedestals with the diya glow below.

---

VIDEO 7 — Grand Finale (Gift It)
Begin with the arc of sweets. Camera slowly pushes forward.

Each sweet flies into the open BR-Mithai jewel box, landing in its gold-foil cup. The lid closes slowly with a soft magnetic close. A gold silk ribbon ties itself into a bow. The box rotates 30 degrees to its hero angle. Distant diyas glow in bokeh and a few gentle sparkler trails arc across the far background.

End frame: Closed, ribboned BR-Mithai box centered with generous negative space for headline and "Send a Box" call-to-action.
BR-Mithai Website Specification (Claude Code Prompt) Website Brief · Next.js / GSAP ScrollTrigger / Lenis / R3F

Master developer prompt instructing Claude Code to build BR-Mithai with Next.js App Router, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis, scroll-lit diya particles, vark-glint shaders, 7 scroll-scrubbed video chapters, and a "Send a Box" gift card builder.

You are an award-winning creative developer.

You think like the teams behind Apple, Active Theory, Bruno Simon, Resn, Locomotive, Dogstudio and Awwwards Site of the Day winners.

Your task is to build a world-class cinematic Diwali website for a fictional luxury Indian sweets brand named

BR-Mithai

Theme: "Diwali in 3D". The website must NOT feel like an ecommerce website. It should feel like opening a jewellery box: an interactive festive film controlled entirely by scrolling with buttery-smooth physics. No generic sections, no bootstrap grids, no cards everywhere.

==================================================
1. TECH STACK
==================================================
- Next.js latest (App Router)
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- GSAP 3 & GSAP ScrollTrigger
- Lenis smooth scroll
- Framer Motion
- React Three Fiber (@react-three/fiber) & Drei (@react-three/drei)
- SplitType
- React Icons

==================================================
2. DESIGN SYSTEM & PALETTE
==================================================
- Festive luxury editorial: Sabyasachi lookbook meets Cartier jewellery launch meets Apple keynote.
- Base Background: Deep maroon velvet (#3A0A12) fading to near-black (#120306), with sections warming to saffron and gold as diyas light up.
- Brand Colors:
  * Velvet Maroon: #3A0A12
  * Night: #120306
  * Temple Gold: #D4A24C
  * Bright Gold: #F2C76E
  * Saffron: #FF8A1F
  * Marigold: #FFB300
  * Silver Vark: #E6E8EC
  * Pistachio: #9DBF6B
  * Ivory: #FBF4E6
- Typography: Canela / Playfair Display for huge serif headlines, Satoshi / Inter for body and micro labels, plus a Devanagari accent font (Tiro Devanagari Hindi) for small Hindi words such as "शुभ दीपावली" and "मिठाई".
- Decorative details: thin gold paisley line-art borders, subtle velvet noise texture, gold-foil gradient strokes on headlines.

==================================================
3. LIVING, GOLD-FOIL TYPOGRAPHY
==================================================
- Split headlines into characters with SplitType. Characters fade up from darkness and catch a sweeping gold highlight (animated gradient mask) as they enter.
- Multi-colored headline styling:
  * "OPEN" (ivory) / "THE" (thin gold outline) / "CELEBRATION" (gold foil)
  * "KAJU" (silver) / "KATLI" (gold)
  * "A THOUSAND" (saffron) / "PEARLS" (gold)
  * "SWEET" (saffron) / "SPIRALS" (marigold)
  * "LIGHT" (gold) / "EVERY" (ivory) / "CORNER" (saffron)
  * "SEND" (ivory) / "SWEETNESS" (gold foil)
- Giant 220px outline watermark text behind each sweet ("मिठाई", "DIWALI", "LADOO") at 8% opacity, drifting in parallax.

==================================================
4. VIDEO SYSTEM & SCROLL-SCRUBBING
==================================================
Videos stored in `assets/videos/`:
- `01-jewel-box.mp4`
- `02-kaju-katli.mp4`
- `03-motichoor-ladoo.mp4`
- `04-jalebi.mp4`
- `05-diya-rangoli.mp4`
- `06-collection.mp4`
- `07-finale.mp4`

All videos fill the viewport inside pinned GSAP ScrollTrigger sections. Video progress (`video.currentTime`) is strictly bound to scroll progress. No autoplay, no native controls, no audio. Preload all videos as blobs and scrub with requestAnimationFrame for zero-lag seeking.

==================================================
5. SCROLL-LIT DIYA SYSTEM (signature interaction)
==================================================
- A fixed React Three Fiber canvas layered over the whole page holds 120 tiny diya flame particles placed along the page edges and around headlines.
- All flames start unlit. As overall scroll progress increases, diyas ignite one by one (staggered by scroll position) with a small flicker-in, so by the finale the whole page glows.
- Each flame uses a small custom shader: flickering noise, additive blending, soft bloom (postprocessing Bloom).
- A live counter in the corner: "Diyas lit: 37 / 120".
- Cursor acts as a matchstick: hovering near an unlit diya lights it early with a tiny spark burst.

==================================================
6. 7 SCROLL CHAPTERS
==================================================

SECTION 1 — HERO (THE JEWEL BOX):
Pinned `01-jewel-box.mp4`. The lid opens as you scroll. Headline: "OPEN THE CELEBRATION". Subtitle: "Handcrafted mithai, boxed like jewellery." Small Devanagari line above: "शुभ दीपावली". CTA: "Explore the Box". Light from the opening lid spills into the UI as a radial gold gradient whose intensity follows scroll.

SECTION 2 — KAJU KATLI:
Pinned `02-kaju-katli.mp4`. Headline: "KAJU KATLI". Staggered callouts that slide in from the sides: "Cashew", "Pure Ghee", "Silver Vark", "Hand-cut Diamonds". A cursor-follow specular highlight makes the vark glint on hover.

SECTION 3 — MOTICHOOR LADOO:
Pinned `03-motichoor-ladoo.mp4`. Headline: "A THOUSAND PEARLS". Animated counter from 0 to 1,000 ("boondi in every ladoo"). Floating saffron pearl particles react to scroll velocity.

SECTION 4 — JALEBI:
Pinned `04-jalebi.mp4`. Headline: "SWEET SPIRALS". An SVG spiral path draws itself in gold (MotionPath / stroke-dashoffset) in sync with the video, with a syrup-drip micro-animation at the end of the stroke.

SECTION 5 — DIYA RANGOLI:
Pinned `05-diya-rangoli.mp4`. Headline: "LIGHT EVERY CORNER". This is where the scroll-lit diya system peaks: a burst lights 40 diyas at once and the page background warms from maroon to saffron. Marigold petals fall in the R3F layer.

SECTION 6 — THE COLLECTION:
Pinned `06-collection.mp4`, then unpin into a horizontal-scroll gallery of 9 sweets: Kaju Katli, Motichoor Ladoo, Jalebi, Gulab Jamun, Rasgulla, Soan Papdi, Pista Barfi, Kesar Peda, Dry-Fruit Roll. Each item is a 3D-tilt card (no boxy borders, just the sweet on a gold pedestal) with name, Hindi name, price per box and a "Add to box" button.

SECTION 7 — GRAND FINALE (SEND A BOX):
Pinned `07-finale.mp4`: sweets fly back into the box and the ribbon ties itself. Headline: "SEND SWEETNESS". Below it, an interactive gift-card builder:
  * Choose box size: 9 / 16 / 25 pieces (box preview updates its compartment grid live).
  * Type a recipient name and a short message; it appears in gold foil on a 3D card that tilts with the cursor.
  * "Send a Box" button and a "Share your greeting" button that exports the card as a 1080x1350 PNG (html-to-image) for Instagram.
Minimal footer with gold paisley divider.

==================================================
7. MICRO-INTERACTIONS & PERFORMANCE
==================================================
- Preloader: a single diya lights up, and its flame grows into a percentage counter in gold.
- Custom cursor: a small glowing flame that leaves a faint sparkle trail; it grows over clickable targets.
- Magnetic buttons with gold-foil sweep on hover.
- Optional ambient sound toggle (soft temple bells + sitar loop), off by default.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: replace video scrubbing with key still frames and disable particles.
- 90+ Lighthouse performance: compressed H.264 + WebM videos, poster frames, IntersectionObserver preloading, capped device pixel ratio for the R3F canvas, GPU-only transforms, fully responsive desktop/tablet/mobile layouts (mobile uses vertical stacking for the collection gallery).

Deliver a production-ready, clean TypeScript codebase ready to run locally via `npm run dev`.
