Build an Ultra-Premium 3D Automotive Website

You are an award-winning creative developer and 3D web engineer. Build an ultra-premium, Apple-level interactive automotive website using the attached design system and the provided single exploded car animation/video as the primary inspiration. This is not a traditional landing page. It should feel like an immersive product launch experience where the user scrolls through a cinematic story.

Core Objective:
Create a smooth scroll-driven 3D experience where every section reveals another part of the car. The website should feel comparable to:
- Apple Product pages
- Porsche Configurator
- Mercedes EQ launch pages
- BMW Vision concept pages
- Nothing product websites

The experience should feel expensive, futuristic and engineered.

Tech Stack:
- React
- Next.js 15
- TypeScript
- Three.js
- React Three Fiber (@react-three/fiber)
- Drei (@react-three/drei)
- GSAP 3
- ScrollTrigger
- Lenis Smooth Scroll
- Framer Motion
- TailwindCSS

Optimize heavily for performance (60+ FPS target).

IMPORTANT:
The provided video is only one exploded animation inspiration. Do not simply replay that video. Instead, study the explosion sequence and recreate the entire experience using actual 3D components. Generate additional missing components using AI or procedural modelling where required. If assets are unavailable, generate premium placeholder 3D meshes that match the design language. The website should feel like it was built from an actual CAD assembly.

3D REQUIREMENTS & COMPONENT BREAKDOWN
==================================================
Create a complete modular car consisting of unmerged 3D objects:
Chassis, Body Shell, Hood, Doors, Front Bumper, Rear Bumper, Roof, Headlights, Tail Lights, Dashboard, Steering Wheel, Seats, Wheels, Tires, Brake Discs, Brake Calipers, Suspension, Exhaust, Battery Pack, Electric Motor, Transmission, Glass, Mirrors, Interior Panels.

Every component should exist as its own 3D object. Nothing should be one merged mesh.

==================================================
9-CHAPTER SCROLL EXPERIENCE NARRATIVE
==================================================

Hero (Scene 1):
Car slowly emerges from darkness. Only headlights illuminate. Tiny floating dust particles. Minimal typography. Camera slowly rotates around front quarter.

Section 2 (Full CAD Explosion):
As scrolling begins, the entire car explodes into hundreds of perfectly aligned parts. Every part floats with smooth physics. Camera moves between components. Text explains precision engineering.

Section 3 (Body Shell & Aerodynamics):
Body shell rotates. Doors float outward. Glass lifts. Roof separates. Panels reveal internal titanium spaceframe structure.

Section 4 (Battery Pack & Powertrain Reveal):
Battery pack slides out from underneath. Electric motors rotate along axis. Energy pulses travel through high-voltage cables with animated glowing energy lines.

Section 5 (Suspension & Brake Kinetics):
Suspension breakdown. Wheel assembly separates. Brake discs rotate. Calipers animate clamping action. Shock absorbers expand. Micro interactions on every component.

Section 6 (Interior Architecture & Tech):
Interior reveal. Seats rise. Dashboard unfolds. Ambient LED lighting appears. Steering wheel rotates into place. Digital screens activate with boot sequence.

Section 7 (Performance Core):
Performance section. Camera enters engine bay. Mechanical components animate at high speed. Exploded assembly reforms. Particles follow movement vectors.

Section 8 (Synchronized Vehicle Rebuild):
Entire vehicle rebuilds itself. Every single component flies back together with perfect synchronization in slow motion with a premium cinematic camera push.

Final Section (Studio Showcase & Configurator CTA):
Finished vehicle rotates slowly. Background transforms into an endless premium studio with a reflection floor, soft volumetric lights, and interactive CTA button ("CONFIGURE YOUR VEHICLE").
