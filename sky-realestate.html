Sky Real Estate Architectural Exhibition Website Spec Website Brief · React + Vite / GSAP

Complete specification instructing AI coding agents to build an Awwwards-level interactive architectural experience for a luxury skyscraper gated community using React + Vite, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis, and TailwindCSS.

2. Claude/Antigravity :- This is exactly the kind of project that can look like an Awwwards Site of the Day if executed correctly.

A few implementation details that will elevate it further

* Hero: Since your hero asset is 9:16 while the rest are 16:9, don’t stretch it. Place the portrait video in the center with a subtle white glow and let it occupy about 45–55% of the viewport width on desktop. The surrounding whitespace will make it feel premium rather than empty.
* Section transitions: Instead of hard cuts, morph the background and typography smoothly so each section feels like a continuation of the story.
* Motion philosophy: Favor slow, confident movement with generous easing. Avoid fast animations; luxury brands communicate quality through restraint.
* Micro-interactions: Use magnetic buttons, subtle cursor scaling, gentle card tilt, and soft glass highlights rather than flashy effects.

This structure will make the experience feel much closer to an architectural showcase than a conventional real-estate landing page, while making the most of the portrait hero asset and landscape supporting content.
